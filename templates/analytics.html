AI Executive Brief
01

Contract Length is the #1 Retention Factor

Month-to-month subscribers account for over 88% of all churn events. Transitioning month-to-month users into 1-year plans reduces attrition risk by more than 60%.

02

First Year (0-12 Months) Critical Drop-off

Subscribers with tenure under 12 months experience the steepest attrition. Onboarding support and proactive satisfaction touchpoints during months 1-3 yield maximum ROI.

03

Electronic Check Vulnerability

Customers paying with electronic checks churn at twice the rate of automated credit card or bank transfer users. Incentivizing auto-pay adoption stabilizes recurring revenue.
