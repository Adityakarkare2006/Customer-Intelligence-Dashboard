PROJECT

Project Overview

Customer Intelligence Dashboard is an enterprise-grade AI analytics and churn prediction web application. Built on Python Flask and Scikit-Learn, it bridges machine learning model inference with interactive business intelligence dashboards.

The platform enables marketing and customer success teams to identify at-risk subscribers before they leave, analyze behavioral patterns across demographics and contracts, and execute targeted retention strategies.
